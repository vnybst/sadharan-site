Sadharan Pause — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

Sadharan Pause is a simple, dark app that lets you decide when other apps may be opened, made by Sadharan ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information on your phone.

In short: everything stays on your phone. Pause has no internet access at all, so it can't send anything anywhere. It has no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.

What the app reads

  • Which app is on screen. With your permission (Usage access), Pause sees which app comes to the front, so it can show its pause screen when you open an app you've paused. It sees only which app it is and when, never what you do inside it.
  • Your phone's own record of app use. The same permission lets Pause read how long each app was open, from the record Android already keeps (usually about the last 10 days). Pause uses it for the Stats tab.
  • The apps you can open. Pause lists the apps on your home screen (their names and icons), so you can pick which to pause.

What the app stores, and where

All of the following is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Your rules. Which apps are paused, and how: paused completely, open only at times you chose, a break after each use, or your default rule. For an app with a break, when you last left it.
  • Your choices. For example how "Open anyway" works, and whether Pause is on.
  • What Pause did. Each time the pause screen came up, and whether you chose "Open anyway". Kept for 60 days, for the Stats tab.
  • Daily app times. How long each app was open on each day, saved before Android's own record forgets it, so the Stats tab can compare one week with the week before. This covers every app you used, not only paused ones, so the comparison can include time from before you paused an app. Kept for 60 days.

What leaves the phone

Nothing, by itself. Pause has no internet permission. Two buttons in Settings hand you over to other apps:

  • Send feedback opens your email app with our address and the app version filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send there. We use the emails we receive only to reply and to improve the app, and don't share them.
  • Rate Pause opens the Play Store.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
Usage accessTo see which app comes to the front, and how long apps were open (for Stats). You turn it on in Android's settings; Pause asks first and explains why.
Display over other appsTo bring up the pause screen over a paused app. Pause shows nothing else over other apps. You turn it on in Android's settings.
Run in the foregroundPause keeps running while any app is paused, so it can step in. Android shows a quiet notification while it does.
Notifications (optional)For that one notification. It never makes a sound. Pause works without it.
Run at startupSo paused apps stay paused after the phone restarts.

Pause asks for nothing else: no internet, contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage or accounts. From Settings it can open Android's battery and app settings, where you can let it keep running; it doesn't change them itself.

Backup

Pause's information is not included in Android's cloud backup or when you move to a new phone, so it never leaves this phone that way either. A new phone starts fresh.

Keeping and deleting your information

Everything stays on your phone until you remove it. You can unpause apps, clear the Stats tab's records with Clear stats in Settings, or delete everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Feedback emails you sent us can be deleted on request.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't collect information from anyone, including children.
