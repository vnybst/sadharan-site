Sadharan Money — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026 (crash report deletion added 7 October 2026)

Sadharan Money is a simple, dark app for noting what you spend, made by Sadharan ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information on your phone.

In short: your accounts, entries and budgets stay on your phone. The app has no sign-up, no ads and no analytics, and never sends your money information anywhere. The one thing that leaves your phone is an anonymous crash report, if the app crashes, as described below.

What the app stores, and where

All of the following is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Your money information. Accounts and their balances, income and expense entries (amount, category, date, note), transfers, monthly budgets and monthly repeating entries.
  • Your choices. For example the currency you picked and whether you finished the first screen.

What leaves the phone

  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics (a Google service that is part of the app) sends a report to Google so we can fix the problem. The report contains technical information only: the app version, your phone model and Android version, what the app was doing when it failed (a stack trace), and a random identifier for the installation. It does not contain your accounts, amounts or notes. Google processes it under Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase's terms. Crash reports are used only to fix bugs.
  • Send feedback opens your email app with our address, a subject line and the app version filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send there. We use the emails we receive only to reply and to improve the app, and don't share them.
  • Rate the app opens the Play Store.
  • Backup to a file. You can save all your data as one file, anywhere you choose, using Android's file picker. The file is not encrypted and holds everything you have entered, so keep it somewhere you trust. We never see it. Restore reads a file you choose, the same way.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
Internet and network stateUsed only by Firebase Crashlytics to send crash reports. The app's own code makes no network calls.

The app asks for nothing else: no contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage or notifications.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. This is handled by Android and Google under your backup settings, and can be turned off in your phone's settings. We have no access to it.

Keeping and deleting your information

Everything stays on your phone until you remove it. You can delete entries, accounts and budgets in the app, or delete everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Backup files you saved are yours to delete. Feedback emails you sent us can be deleted on request.

Crash reports. A crash report has no name, email or account in it, so we can't pick out one person's reports. Google keeps them for about 90 days and then deletes them automatically. Uninstalling the app stops any more being sent. If you want us to delete crash data from your phone sooner, write to apps.androhub@gmail.com and tell us roughly when the app crashed and your phone model; we'll remove what we can match and tell you what we could not.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
